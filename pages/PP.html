4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience, analyze platform usage, and provide personalized services. This section explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can control them.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites or use apps. They help us remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and provide a personalized experience. We use both session cookies (deleted when you close your browser) and persistent cookies (remain until they expire or you delete them).

Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration Required
Essential Cookies Enable core platform functionality, authentication, and security features Session/30 days Yes
Performance Cookies Analyze platform usage, identify errors, and improve performance 90 days No
Functional Cookies Remember your preferences, language settings, and customizations 1 year No
Analytics Cookies Understand user behavior, popular features, and platform effectiveness 2 years No
Marketing Cookies Deliver relevant advertisements and measure campaign effectiveness 1 year No

Third-Party Cookies

We may allow trusted third-party services to place cookies on your device for:

  • Google Analytics: Website traffic analysis and user behavior insights
  • Social Media Plugins: Social sharing functionality and login options
  • Payment Processors: Secure transaction processing and fraud prevention
  • Customer Support: Live chat functionality and help desk services
  • Advertising Partners: Relevant ad delivery and campaign measurement

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You're in control: You can manage cookies through several methods:

  • Browser Settings: Most browsers allow you to view, delete, and block cookies
  • Cookie Preference Center: Access our cookie settings through your account preferences
  • Opt-Out Tools: Use third-party opt-out tools for advertising cookies
  • Mobile Settings: Adjust tracking preferences in your device settings

Please note: Disabling essential cookies may affect platform functionality and your ability to use certain features.

Other Tracking Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use:

  • Web Beacons: Small invisible images that help us understand email engagement
  • Local Storage: Browser storage for offline functionality and performance
  • Device Fingerprinting: Security measure to detect fraudulent activities
  • App Analytics: Mobile app usage tracking for performance optimization
